Airlift Privacy Policy

App: Airlift (Android, package com.airlift.app)
Effective date: October 25, 2026

Airlift is published by Aurelius Thesara, a brand of Aurelious Enterprises LLC, Richmond, Texas, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Airlift app handles and how.

What Airlift does

Airlift backs up photos, videos, audio, and contacts from your phone to your own paired Windows PC running the Airlift PC companion. We do not provide cloud storage. We do not receive, store, or have access to your content.

Pairing

Your phone is paired with your PC by scanning a QR code. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code.

How data is transferred

  • Transfers happen over your local network using TLS encryption pinned to your paired PC.
  • Optional Remote Access (off unless you enable it) routes encrypted traffic through a relay at airliftlink.duckdns.org so your phone can reach your PC when you are away from home. The relay forwards encrypted traffic and is not used to store your content. The same server also provides app update and availability information.

No tracking, ads, or data sharing

  • Airlift contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.
  • Airlift shows no ads.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Photos, videos, and audio (media read): to back up and browse your media.
  • Contacts (optional): to back up your contacts.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code.
  • Notifications: to show backup status.
  • Foreground data-sync service and battery-optimization exemption: to run backups reliably in the background.
  • Network, Wi-Fi, and multicast: to find and communicate with your PC on your local network.
  • Boot completed: to resume scheduled backups after your phone restarts.

Private vault

Airlift offers an optional encrypted private vault protected by a recovery phrase you create. We cannot recover the vault or your recovery phrase if you lose it.

Free up space

The "Free up space" feature deletes copies from your phone only after your PC confirms it has them.

Your data and deletion

You control all of your data. Uninstalling the app and deleting the backed-up files on your PC removes it. Unpairing stops all transfers. For questions or requests, contact us at the email below.

Children

Airlift is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
